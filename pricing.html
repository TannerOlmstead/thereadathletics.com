Institution Pricing

Pricing is based on the number of active sports. Each activated sport receives a dedicated recruiting workspace with up to 6 users, plus the features below.

Included With Each Activated Sport

  • Dedicated recruiting workspace
  • Up to 6 users
  • Recruiting board
  • Shared messaging
  • Shared tasks
  • AI access
  • Athlete search
  • Evaluations
  • Analytics
  • Reporting

Institution Features

  • Shared recruiting board and staff evaluations
  • Shared messaging, reports, tasks, and calendar
  • Team needs, Coach Fit, Adjusted Recruit Score, Priority Score
  • Commitments, analytics, regional insights
  • THE READ AI
  • Institutional data ownership

Annual billing = 10 monthly payments, so institutions get two months free by paying annually. Additional user pricing is to be announced.
