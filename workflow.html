The Recruiting Workflow.

Built in the coach workspace today (demo, localStorage, sample data).

  1. Master Database
    Shared list of prospects. College coaches search it and add from their offline board. Calculated scores are read-only.
  2. Add To Board
    The green (+) button adds a prospect to your program’s own Recruiting Board. Boards are per program.
  3. Coach Fit & Team Needs
    Coach Fit and Team Need scores set the Priority Tier automatically.
  4. Coach Grading
    Each signed-in coach grades with A+ to F or 0–100. The Head Coach sets staff weights, by position group if wanted.
  5. Adjusted Recruit Score
    Updates as grades arrive. It never changes Tier or Confidence.
  6. Connect
    Message athletes, coaches and instructors. Demo only: messages are not sent and nothing is encrypted yet.
  7. Calendar & Schedule Visit
    Scheduling a visit creates a calendar event and logs the action.
  8. Automatic Stage
    Stage comes from logged actions, never picked by hand: Identified, Contacted, Evaluating, Visit Planned, Visit Completed, Offer Out, Committed (or Declined if it is the latest action).
  9. Commitments
    Committed prospects appear on the Commitments page with school rankings.
  • JUCO and transfer portal: every player list shows Level / Class (HS 2027, JUCO 2028, Tr 2029) and Eligibility Left, which coaches or athletes enter.
  • Current Playing Level is a field on every prospect, with a Level filter.

Workflow By Role.

What each role does, sees, and connects with. “Coming Soon” means not built yet.

Athlete

  • Does: creates a profile, adds a photo, enters metrics, chooses what is shared.
  • Sees: own player card and athlete calendar.
  • Connects: messages from coaches through Connect (demo); the athlete controls who can message.
  • Verified data: each metric shows who verified it, or “Unverified / Coach-Entered”.

Parent / Guardian (Minors)

  • Does: consents to visibility and contact for athletes under 18.
  • Sees: what programs can see; message oversight.
  • Coming Soon Guardian accounts and under-18 contact rules need a backend and compliance review.

High School Coach

  • Does: verifies athlete metrics and vouches for athletes.
  • Sees: their athletes and Connect threads.
  • Connects: messages college coaches and athletes (demo).

Coach sign-up →

Private Instructor / Trainer

  • Does: verifies metrics from lessons and training, and introduces athletes to coaches.
  • Sees: athletes they verify.
  • Connects: Connect directory and threads (demo).

Verified Data Provider

  • Does: showcases, events and measurement providers verify metrics such as velocity and exit speed.
  • Shows up as: the verifier (name, role, organization, date) on the player card Connect panel.
  • Coming Soon Automated data imports.

JUCO Coach

  • Does: lists JUCO players and eligibility, and follows transfer-portal movement.
  • Sees: Connect, Level / Class and Eligibility Left.
  • Connects: 4-year coaches and athletes (demo).

4-Year College Coach

  • Head Coach: sets grade weights, grading scale and team needs.
  • Pitching / Position / Assistant Coaches, Recruiting Coordinator, Graduate Staff: each signs in as themselves, grades prospects, and logs actions.
  • All staff: use the Master Database, board, Coach Fit, Connect and Calendar.
