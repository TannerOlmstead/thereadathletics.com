How to build a recruiting board that works

A simple five-stage board and the few fields that actually matter.

THE READ Athletics · Starter article · General information only

Start with five stages

Most programs only need Discovered, Contacted, Visit, Offer, and Committed. More stages usually mean more clutter and less updating.

Track fewer fields, better

  • Position and class
  • One or two verified measurables
  • A single owner on your staff
  • The next step and its due date

Review weekly

Spend fifteen minutes each week moving athletes forward or off the board. A board nobody trusts is worse than none.
