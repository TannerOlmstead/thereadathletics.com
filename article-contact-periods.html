Contact and dead periods: a plain-language primer

Why recruiting calendars matter and how to build a habit of checking them.

THE READ Athletics · Starter article · General information only

Why calendars matter

College recruiting rules limit when and how coaches may contact athletes. The limits differ by sport, division, and year.

Build a habit

  • Check the official NCAA or NAIA calendar for your sport before outreach.
  • Log every contact with date and method.
  • Ask your compliance office when unsure.
Not compliance advice. This is a general overview. Always rely on official sources and your institution’s compliance staff.
